Send to Town — settings

From your Web Capture Inbox routine in Town → Config → Webhook → Enable.

A comma-separated shortcut list for the popup’s “File into” menu — your own labels, not a read of your Town library, so any name works (existing or new). Town always files into a type subfolder (reading / jobs / social / shopping / other) inside the collection you pick; on “Auto” it uses captures/<type>.

Pre-selects this collection in the popup. Blank keeps every capture on Auto until you choose one.

Beyond filing a capture, you can ask your Townie to act on it. Press Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+K on any page (or right-click → Ask Town to do something…) and a small box opens at your selection. Type something like “draft a reply asking about the storage fee”. The popup has a matching field.

Town files the capture as usual, then hands your instruction to a separate task, which shows up in Town and notifies you when it is done.

Notes and instructions are different. A note (and the quick-action chips) is filing context — why you saved it, where it goes. It never makes anything happen. An instruction asks for actual work. Keeping them apart is deliberate: page text can never turn itself into an instruction, so only what you type can direct anything.

What a task may do: draft emails (drafts only, left for you to review), write documents and notes, research, and hold time on your own calendar. What it may not do: send or reply to email, message anyone, RSVP, or anything else visible outside your account. Nothing reaches another person unless you click send. Instructions are skipped on all-tabs sweeps and logged in your capture log instead.

  • Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+S — send the current page
  • Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+E — send every open tab
  • Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+K — open the Ask Town box

New shortcuts sometimes arrive unassigned, or collide with something your browser already owns. If one does nothing, set it here.

The extension ships with a neutral default icon. To make the button your own Townie: open your Town assistant settings, save your Townie's picture, then upload it below (or paste its URL). Leave blank to keep the neutral default.
