Welcome to Send to Town

Capture a page, your highlight, or every open tab — your Townie sorts it into your Content Library.

1 Add the Web Capture Inbox routine

This is the Town routine that receives your captures, classifies them, and files them. Install it once, then enable its webhook.

Open Town routines

2 Connect your webhook

In the routine's Config → Webhook, click Enable, then paste the URL and secret here.

3 Pick your collections

Comma-separated shortcuts for the popup’s “File into” menu — your own labels, not a read of your Town library, so any name works. Town always adds a type subfolder (reading / jobs / social / shopping / other) inside the collection you pick; leave a capture on “Auto” and it lands in captures/<type>.

4 Make the icon your Townie

The toolbar button ships with a neutral default. Make it your Townie so your captures feel like yours.

Where to find your Townie image: open your Town assistant settings, save your Townie's picture, then upload it below (or paste a direct image URL if you have one).

5 You're set

  • Click the toolbar button, add an optional note or quick-action, and hit Send this page.
  • Highlight text and right-click → Send selection to Town.
  • Ask your Townie to do something with a page: Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+K, or right-click → Ask Town to do something….

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+S — send the current page
  • Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+E — send every open tab
  • Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+K — open the Ask Town box

A new shortcut sometimes arrives unassigned, or collides with something your browser already owns. If one does nothing, set it here.

Quick actions are one-tap presets in the popup (Summarize, Read later, Add todo, Reference). They just add a short hint to your note — tapping Summarize and Add todo sends the note “summarize; add a todo” — which your Townie reads as a hint for how to file the capture. Tap as many as you like, or type your own. (“Add todo” records a checkbox in your capture log, not a separate Town task.)

Notes vs instructions. A note is filing context — why you saved it, where it goes — and never makes anything happen. An instruction asks for real work: “draft a reply asking about the storage fee”. Type one in the Ask Town box or the popup’s matching field, and Town files the capture as usual, then hands your instruction to a separate task that notifies you when it’s done. Keeping the two apart is deliberate — page text can never turn itself into an instruction, so only what you type directs anything.

A task may draft emails (drafts only, for you to review), write documents, research, and hold time on your own calendar. It may not send email, message anyone, or do anything else visible outside your account — nothing reaches another person unless you click send.
